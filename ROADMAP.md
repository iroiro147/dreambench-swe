# Public Roadmap

This roadmap describes directions, not promises or benchmark results.

## Near term

- Validate the public artifact on clean macOS and Linux environments.
- Improve quick-start diagnostics and failure messages.
- Publish the canonical paper identifier and update `CITATION.cff`.
- Collect independent reproducibility reports and methodology critiques.

## Candidate extensions

- Additional multi-session maintenance traps with explicit admission evidence.
- Cross-model and cross-agent adapter coverage without weakening comparability.
- Better contamination and benchmark-leakage diagnostics.
- Community-authored analyzers and visualization tools over the public folded
  outputs.

## Governance principle

Changes to scoring, trap admission, canonical datasets, or headline claims need
public rationale, regression evidence, and a versioned release boundary. New
experiments do not silently overwrite prior results.
